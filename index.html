Key Executive Findings & Recommendations
  • Discount Cap Recommendation: Discounts between 0%–20% generate healthy 29.3%–40.0% profit margins. Discounts >60% incurred -$1.55M in net losses (-137.1% margin). Enforce a 20% discount cap.
  • Top Product Category: Technology Copiers in the South & West regions represent the single largest revenue driver ($1.14M+ per region).
  • Customer Churn: Over 73% of historical customers have reached inactive churn status (>180 days since last order). Target At-Risk customers with automated re-engagement offers.
